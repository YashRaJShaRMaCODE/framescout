# FrameScout market research

Checked 2026-11-17. Public prices can vary by country and app store. Review themes below are examples from self-selected public reviews, not representative research or proof that FrameScout is better.

## User and problem hypothesis

Likely first user: a film fan or small film club that wants to find a worthwhile movie without browsing several catalogs, and keep a private list that can be backed up. The repo currently offers a free catalog, filters, related picks, shareable collection URLs, and a device-local watchlist. There is no account, streaming-availability lookup, or validated paying buyer.

**Positioning:** FrameScout is a focused film discovery catalog with fast filters, shareable collections, and a private watchlist that stays on the user's device.

**Possible paid pilot, unvalidated:** a one-time curated catalog or themed screening collection for a film club. Do not price or promise this before interviews and content-rights review.

## Rights and evidence limits

- The current catalog and artwork must be checked against each data source's license before commercial use. TMDB's API is free for non-commercial use with required attribution; commercial use needs separate permission. [TMDB API FAQ](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq)
- JustWatch's terms describe personal, non-commercial use; its partner API requires a contract/token and branded links. [Terms](https://support.justwatch.com/article/just-watchs-terms-of-use), [partner API docs](https://apis.justwatch.com/docs/api/)
- Do not scrape competitor catalogs, copy posters/reviews, or infer that publicly visible data grants resale rights.
- Public store/forum reviews are anecdotal, region-dependent, and may be outdated. No target-user interviews or willingness-to-pay evidence are in this repo.
