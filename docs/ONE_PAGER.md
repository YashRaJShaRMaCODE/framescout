# FrameScout — product brief

## Current product

A Django-rendered film catalog for browsing by genre, year, director, and score. Users can share public filtered collections and keep a watchlist on their own device. It has no account sync, streaming provider availability, paid plan, or validated revenue.

## User and problem hypothesis

Film fans and small film clubs may lose time comparing titles across catalog sites when choosing a movie. This is a hypothesis; no customer interviews are recorded yet.

## Differentiation to validate

Fast, understandable filters, explainable related picks, a private device-local watchlist, and a clean share link. Do not claim superiority before user trials.

## Pilot hypothesis

Offer one-time curation of a rights-cleared themed collection for a film club. Define buyer, scope, success measure, price, support, and data rights only after interviews. No outreach has been sent and no price is approved.

## Risks and next proof

1. Resolve commercial metadata/poster rights before any paid catalog use.
2. Restore and verify the public backend; it previously returned HTTP 503.
3. Interview five film fans and three organizers; document repeated pain and a concrete pilot decision.
4. Prove backup/restore, monitoring, privacy/legal notices, and a full mobile journey before launch.
